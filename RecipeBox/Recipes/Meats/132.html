Homemade Southern style meat loaf

Ingredients:

  • 1 1/2 pounds ground beef, 85 percent lean
  • 20 saltine crackers, crushed
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped onion
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped green bell pepper
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1 teaspoon paprika (I think Mom used tobasco sauce)
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme leaves
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1/3 cup ketchup
  • 2 tablespoons milk (? dont think Mom used milk)
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
Topping:
  • 1/2 to 3/4 cup ketchup or barbecue sauce
Directions:
  1. Heat the oven to 350. Grease an 8 1/2 X 11 inch loaf pan or lline with foil and lightly grease.
  2. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients except the topping.
  3. Pack the meat mixture into the loaf pan and bake for one hour. Carefully pour off excess fat. Spread with the 3/4 cup ketchup or barbecue sauce and continue baking for 15 minutes.

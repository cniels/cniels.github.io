Ranchburger

1 lb. ground beef
Dice 1/2 cup celery
1/2 cup onion
1/4 cup mangos
1 can tomato soup
1 T. salt, dash pepper and garlic to season
1 T. Worcestershire Sauce
1 T. vinegar
1 T. sugar
Cook meat until no longer red. Drain grease, add
tomato soup, diced ingredients and seasoning. Cook 325
until vegetables are colored.

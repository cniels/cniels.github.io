Hamburger Chop Suey

Hamburger Chop Suey
Serves 6
1 1/2 1 lb. Hamburger
2 med. onion, thinly sliced
5 stalks celery diced
1 #10 can bean sprouts drained
1 cup beef boulon
3/4 to 1 1/2 tsp salt
1/4 tsp. pepper
3 T. cornstarch
1 T. brown sugar
1 to 1 1/2 T. soy sauce
2 T. water
1 can Chinese Noodles

Brown hamburger, onions: Add celery, bouillion, salt, and pepper. Cover and cook 10 min. Make a smooth paste of cornstarch, sugar and soy sauce. Stir into meat and cook another 10 min. or until sauce is thick and clean. Add bean sprouts; Cook untilsprouts are heated. Serve over noodles. Yield 6 servings.

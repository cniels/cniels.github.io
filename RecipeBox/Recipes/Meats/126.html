Ranchburger

1 lb.     ground beef
Dice     1/2 cup celery
        1/2 cup onion
        1/4 cup mangoes
1 T. Worcestershire S.
1 T. vinegar
1 T. sugar
1 can tomato soup
Dash pepper and garlic to season
Cook meat until no longer red.
Drain grease, add tomato soup, diced ingredients and seasoning. Cook 325° until vegetables are colored.

Note 5/99 - cut sugar

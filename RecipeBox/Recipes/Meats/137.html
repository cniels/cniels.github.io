Fresh Beef Brisket in Sauce

1 1/2 tsp. salt
1 1/2 tsp. monosodium glutanate
1 1/2 tsp. pepper
1 T. chili powder
1/2 tsp. garlic powder
1 tsp. crushed bay leaves
4 lbs., fresh brisket

Place meat in pan. Sprinkle with seasonings. Bake, covered 3 1/2 hours, 275°. Put sauce on, cook another 30 minutes.

TEXAS BARBEQUE SAUCE
1 cup catsup
1/4 cup cider vinegar
1 tsp. sugar +
1 tsp. chili powder
1 cup broth (or water)
3 stalks celery, chopped
3 bay leaves
1 clove garlic
2 T. chopped onion
4 T. worcestershire sauce
1 tsp. papriki
dash of pepper

Boil, cover, turn heat down and simmer for 15 minutes.

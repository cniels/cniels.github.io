Tacos

Serves: 6-8

Cook:   1 1/2 lbs. hamburger
        1 1/2 onions
        2 tsp. salt
Add     2 T. chile powder
        1 1/2 cans tomato paste and 1 1/2 cans water
        pepper, and 1 tsp. salt
        1/2 tsp. oregano
        1/2 tsp. sweetbasil
        1 1/2 tsp. sugar
        3/4 tsp. garlic
        Simmer all together 20 min.

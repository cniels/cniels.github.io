Texas Brisket

4 lbs. brisket
1 1/2 tsp. salt
1 1/2 tsp. MQS
1 1/2 tsp. pepper
2 T. chili pd.
crushed bayleaves (abt 3)

Place meat in flat pan. Mix seasonings, sprinkle over meat. Bake, covered 3 1/2 hrs. Put sauce on - cool another

1/2 hr. Put about 1 cup water in pan.
Sauce
1 c. catsup
1/2 c. cider vinegar
1 tsp. sugar
1 tsp. chili pd.
1 c. broth
3 stalks celery, diced
3 bay leaves
1 garlic cloves
2 T. chopped onions
4 T. worcheshire sauce
1 tsp. paprika
dash pepper

Boil, turn down heat - simmer 15 min

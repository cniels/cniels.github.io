Spicy Chops And Cabbage

4 pork loin chops
2 T. water
1/2 tsp. salt
2 whole cloves
1/2 of small bay leaf
1 medium head cabbage coarsely shredded (8 cups)
1 1/2 cups diced tart apple
1/4 cup chopped onion
1/4 cup sugar
1 1/2 tsp. all-purpose flour
1/2 tsp. salt
2 T. vinegar
2 T. water
over

trim fat from meat; cook fat in skellet to oil surface. Discard trimmings. Brown chops in skillet. Add the first 2 T. water, the first 1/2 tsp. salt, the cloves, and bay leaf; cover and simmer 30 minutes. Remove chops from skillet; discard cloves and bay leaf. Add cabbage, apple, and onion. Combine sugar, flour, and the second 1/2 teaspoon salt. Stir in vinegar and the remaining water. Pour over cabbage, stirring to mix. Cover and simmer 5 min. Return pork chops to skillet; cover and cook 20 minutes more or till chops and cabbage are tender. Makes 4 servings.

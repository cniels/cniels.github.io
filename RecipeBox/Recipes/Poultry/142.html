Easy Lemon Chicken

1/4 cup olive oil, plus more for brushing chicken
9 garlic cloves, sliced
1/3 cup white wine
1 tablespoon finely grated lemon rind
2 tablespoons lemon juice
3/4 teaspoon dried oregano
1/4 teaspoon dried thyme
1/2 teaspoon salt, divided
2 bone-in, skin-on chicken breasts, or 4 thighs
Freshly ground black pepper
1 lemon, cut into wedges

  1. Preheat oven to 400F.
  2. Heat olive oil in a small skillet over medium heat. Add garlic and cook about 2 minutes. Remove from heat.
  3. Combine wine, lemon rind and juice, oregano, thyme and 1/4 teaspoon salt in a baking dish. Stir well. Pour in oil and garlic. Stir well. Place chicken pieces on top of garlic mixture, skin-side up. Brush skin with a little additional olive oil. Sprinkle with remaining 1/4 teaspoon salt and pepper. Tuck lemonwedges in around chicken. Bake 30 to 35 minutes. Remove from oven and let stand 5 to 10 minutes before serving.
Serves 2

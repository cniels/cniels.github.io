Chicken Taco Filling

Heat oven to 350 degrees F.
INGREDIENTS

  • 6 Boneless Skinless Chicken Thighs (my package had more, but they were small)
  • 1 Cup Salsa Verde
  • 1 Onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
INSTRUCTIONS
  1. Heat oven to 350.
  2. In a dutch oven or glass baking dish arrange chicken thighs in a single layer. Top with salsa, onions, and garlic. (I drizzled a little olive oil in the main_bottom of the baking dish first. Season chicken with salt and pepper, then layer over with garlic, onion slices; pour the salsa over the top.)
  3. Cover and bake for 60-90 minutes or until chicken is fully cooked and onions are translucent. (I did not cover the dish and it did not dry out.)
  4. Shred chicken. Once chicken has been shredded, use a wooden spoon to combine shredded chicken and juices.
  5. Serve as desired. (I like taco style, but you can use this mixture on a baked potato, spaghetti, rice - whatever.)
P.S. We also had beef and taco seasoning. It was do-it-yourself, with lettuce, chopped tomatoes, chopped onions, guacamole, shredded cheese, corn and flour tortillas. Dessert was ice cream and berries.
